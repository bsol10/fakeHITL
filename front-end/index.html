        Common questions

        Why was a source skipped during the scan?

        Usually the page is behind a login wall or the URL has moved. Open Sources, find the row marked URL changed, and update the link. The agent will pick it up on the next scan.

        Can the agent publish on its own?

        No. Every finding waits in Draft review until a staff member approves it. This is fixed by policy and can't be switched off in Settings.

        What does the confidence score mean?

        It's how sure the agent is that a change is real and relevant. Low scores are worth a closer look before you approve them.

        How do I add a new source?

        Go to Sources and choose Add source. Enter the link, topic and how often it updates. A Research Lead signs it off before the agent can use it.
